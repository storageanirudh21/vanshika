HERO SECTION: IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS
======================================

Save each result in public/images/ using the exact filename shown.
All four images must be PNGs with a TRANSPARENT background (no white box).
If your generator can't do transparency, generate on plain white, then run it
through a background remover (remove.bg, Photoshop "Remove Background", or
Magnific / Freepik background removal).

Until a PNG exists, the site automatically falls back to the old .jpg.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Size / ratio: portrait, 2:3 or taller (e.g. 1600 x 2800 px), full body,
head near the very top and shoes at the bottom, small margin around her.

PROMPT:
Full-body studio photograph of a young Indian woman in her mid-20s,
standing and facing the camera with a warm, confident closed-mouth smile.

TIP: For a portfolio of a real person, the best result is a REAL photo of her in
this pose/outfit with the background removed. Use the prompt above as
a shot brief for the photographer.
